How an engagement runs

Four stages, from first audit to ongoing reporting.

  • 01

    Audit

    A review of your current channels and an honest conversation about budget, timeline, and what growth actually needs to look like for you.

  • 02

    Strategy

    Channel selection, messaging, and a content plan presented as a single coherent story — not a checklist of tactics.

  • 03

    Execution

    We run campaigns and content directly, so you get one point of contact for the entire engagement.

  • 04

    Reporting

    Monthly reporting tied to real business outcomes — leads, sales, and retention — not just clicks and impressions.
